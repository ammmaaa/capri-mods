# Mute Button

A mute button next to TYPE and TALK that silences Capri's voice instantly.

- Kind: code
- Made by: Potato

Hover her and a square button shows left of TYPE and TALK, mirroring the mic button in voice chat. Click it and her voice goes silent straight away, even mid-sentence. Her speech bubbles still show. Click it again to hear her at the same volume as before.

- Muted, the button turns red with a crossed-out speaker.
- Moving her volume slider while she's muted unmutes her.
- Turning the mod off or restarting Capri brings her voice back.
- Only her voice is muted. Sound effects and typing sounds still play, and if Capri puts her voice into your mic, it still goes there.
