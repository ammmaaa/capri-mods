# Prism Overdrive

A skin for Capri with every effect, on every frame she has.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Potato

Iridescent hair that flows from magenta through violet and cyan as she moves, with twinkling sparkles. Her panels glow cyan-white and pulse, her dark limbs carry circuit traces with a pulse of light running along them, and a hot-pink rim light, a two-layer bloom and a faint colour split make her light up the desktop. Glowing motes drift up around her, afterimages trail behind her when she moves, and she glitch-flashes now and then (every frame in her own glitch moments). Every pose: standing, walking, sitting, lying down, asleep, dancing and glitching.

New in 1.1.0: she stands in her new 0.8.6 loop in this skin. Needs Capri 0.8.6 or later.
