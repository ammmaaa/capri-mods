# Long Look

Capri watches your screen for about 10 seconds and comments on everything she saw in that stretch, instead of one frame.

- Kind: code
- Made by: Potato

## Say
- **long look** / **take a long look** / **watch this for 20 seconds**: one look, one comment.
- **long look on** / **watch closely with me**: she keeps watching with you, 10 seconds at a time with a rest in between, and remembers her last few lines so she can follow a show.
- **long look off** / **stop watching**: stops.
- **set long look to 20 seconds**: 4 to 30 seconds.

## Good to know
- Each look sends up to 6 small screenshots to the brain you picked for her, in one request. Watching together does that about every 45 seconds, so it uses more of your API quota than her normal glances.
- A small WATCHING sign shows above her while she looks.
- Nothing is taken while a password box is in front, a screen that isn't changing is never sent, and she stops by herself when nothing has moved for a while or after three hours.
- Her own "watch this video with me" is untouched.
