# Kitchen Sponge

A skin for Capri: she's made of a kitchen sponge.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Potato

Her hair and pink panels are yellow sponge foam full of little pores, her green panels are the rough green scrubby pad, and a few soap bubbles float up off her as she moves. Every pose: standing, walking, sitting, lying down, asleep, dancing and glitching.

New in 1.1.0: she stands in her new 0.8.6 loop in this skin. Needs Capri 0.8.6 or later.
