# Study Buddy

A code mod for Capri: she stays her funny self, and gives you a real answer when you ask for one.

- Kind: code
- Made by: Potato

## Ask for a real answer

Put one of these in what you say or type, and she answers that question for real, short and to the point: the correct answer first, then a sentence or two of why (step by step only when maths needs it or you ask).

`genuinely` · `seriously` · `serious question` · `for real` · `no jokes` · `be serious` · `real answer` · `explain properly` · `honest answer`

Her ears don't always catch them, so it also listens for how they get misheard: genuine, genuinly, generally, jenuinely, "genu inly", "june wine lee", seriosly, "serious lee", "sirius lee", "for reel". Typed `srsly` and `fr` work too. Asking for answers counts as well: "tell me the answers", "what's the answer", "answer these questions", "help me with this question", "help me with my homework".

If it's about your screen ("my screen", "this question", "these problems", "look at this"), her brain is told to look at your screen first and then answer what it sees there. A plain "this" or "these" on its own doesn't count as a screen question.

> "Capri, genuinely, what's the derivative of x cubed?"
> "Seriously, what's the answer to this question on my screen?"

A keyword keeps her serious for 90 seconds. The next thing you say without one gets the usual Capri.

## Study mode

Say **"study mode on"** and every answer is a real one, no keyword needed, until you say **"study mode off"**. She remembers it after a restart.

## What she's told

- "Quiz me on photosynthesis" makes her ask one question at a time and tell you if you're right.
- She's asked to check whether what she sees is a live quiz or test (Kahoot, Quizizz, Blooket, Gimkit, Quizlet Live, timed tests, exams) and, if it is, to offer to go over it after the round instead of answering. That's an instruction to her brain, not a block, so it isn't guaranteed.

## How it works

While she's serious it adds one note to what her brain is told, plus a line asking her to look first when you ask about your screen. The look and the answer both go through her brain with that note. It doesn't take screenshots itself, send anything anywhere or change settings, and turning it off removes the note.

It needs a cloud brain that uses its own tools (Capri's default for cloud brains). A local brain doesn't get the note, so she stays her usual self there.
