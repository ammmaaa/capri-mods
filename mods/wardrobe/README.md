# Wardrobe

Wear several skins at once, like Halo over Galaxy Hair, on top of any skin and colour set.

- Kind: code
- Made by: Potato

Say or type:

- "wear halo", "put on galaxy hair", "wear halo and galaxy hair"
- "take off halo"
- "take everything off" (worn pieces only)
- "reset your look", "back to normal", "start fresh" (everything off: worn pieces, the Mods page skin and colour sets, so you can try a new combo from scratch)
- "surprise me", "random outfit", "shuffle your look" (a random fit from the skins and colour sets you have: at most one full skin, up to two smaller pieces, sometimes a colour set, never the same fit twice in a row)
- "what are you wearing?"

Her ears don't always get it right, so it also understands the usual mishearings: "where hello" (wear halo), "tape off hello", "roloffit", "sir prize me", "re set your luck", "whatcha wearing", "galaxy here", "prison over drive", "cyber sian" and more. Skin names that are a letter or two off still match.

The skin you pick on the Mods page stays underneath, and worn pieces go on top. Smaller pieces like Halo always end up on top of bigger ones. Colour sets ("wear cyber cyan") tint her body, and worn pieces keep their own colours.

A new combination takes a few seconds to put together the first time. After that it's saved, so it comes back instantly, even after a restart.
